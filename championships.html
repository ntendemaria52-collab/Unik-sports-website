8th Uganda International Woodball Championships

Dates: 6th to 8th November 2026

Venue: University of Kisubi

Organiser: University of Kisubi, Department of Sports

About Woodball

Woodball is a sport that combines golf-style precision with croquet-style strategy, played across a course of targets.

Format

Teams and individual players compete across rounds, with results scored and ranked at the end of each stage.
